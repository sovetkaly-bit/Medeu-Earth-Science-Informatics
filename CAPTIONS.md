# Figure captions (full text)

### FigA_location_maps

Figure A. Location of the study and validation regions. (a) World overview; (b) Central Asia with the enlarged Kazakhstan window; (c) enlarged window 76.85-77.20°E, 43.05-43.25°N in UTM zone 43N (EPSG:32643). Base layers (a, b): Natural Earth 1:110m / 1:50m admin-0 countries (public domain). Relief in (c): hillshade (azimuth 315°, altitude 45°) of Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (ESA/Copernicus; EGM2008; item dates 2021-04-22) reprojected bilinearly to the 30 m UTM grid; grey = outside the packaged 30 m windows (no data), no relief is simulated there. Coloured rectangles are PROCESSING WINDOWS (analysis extents), not resort territories; solid lines are named OSM polygons where one exists (Medeu: a 0.043 km² sports-centre polygon; Shymbulak: a winter-sports polygon); dashed lines are footprints derived from mapped aerialways/pistes/tourism/leisure features within 2.5-3.5 km of the configured centre (buffer 250 m, erosion 200 m); they are exploratory and are NOT official borders. Kok-Zhailau is shown as two alternative windows: V1 (configured centre, provisional) and V2 (centre from OSM-named objects, ~5.9 km west). Panels a-b have no scale bar because distances are distorted at those scales; panel c has an exact metric scale bar, pyproj graticule and a true-north arrow rotated by the UTM grid convergence.

Background: hillshade of Copernicus GLO-30 DSM at 30 m (EGM2008). ### FigB_decisions_t15

Figure B. Threshold decisions at 15° slope for the Kazakhstan study windows, exactly as classified in the packaged rasters decision_combined_t15_risk2.tif (codes 0 BELOW, 1 ABOVE, 2 ABSTAIN, 255 no decision) produced by the combined (ridge-logistic) abstention rule with the training-risk target 0.02; the rule was trained on lidar-validated sites in Switzerland and the USA and is TRANSFERRED here, so error is not measured at these sites. Background: hillshade of Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (ESA/Copernicus; EGM2008; item dates 2021-04-22) reprojected bilinearly to the 30 m UTM grid. Panels (c) and (d) are two alternative Kok-Zhailau study windows (provisional configured centre vs centre taken from OSM-named objects), kept separate on purpose. Class colours are drawn at 78 % opacity over the hillshade; grey = no decision (nodata). Outlines: dark-blue = OSM-mapped named polygon (exploratory), pink dashed = functional footprint derived from mapped aerialways/pistes/tourism/leisure features (NOT an official boundary); no outline is shown where none exists. Maps are drawn in the metric UTM 43N CRS (EPSG:32643); scale bars are exact lengths in that projection; graticule from pyproj; the arrow points to true north (grid convergence printed). Pixel-to-polygon assignment: pixel centre inside polygon (rasterio rasterize, all_touched=False), masks packaged as mask_*.tif. Per-mask counts, areas, cell size, shares and contour identifiers: tables/map_summary_figB.csv; check against tables/decisions_by_site.csv: tables/verification_figB_vs_decisions_by_site.csv.

### FigC1_kazakhstan_inter_dem_slope_difference

Figure C1. Inter-DEM slope differences (NASADEM minus Copernicus GLO-30) on the common valid mask (pixels with a complete 3x3 neighbourhood in BOTH DEMs) for the four Kazakhstan windows. Both DEMs are DSMs resampled bilinearly to the same 30 m UTM 43N grid; slopes by the Horn (1981) 3x3 method. Differences show MODEL DISAGREEMENT between DEMs, not accuracy: no lidar reference exists for Kazakhstan. ALOS AW3D30 and FABDEM are not mapped because their rasters are not redistributed in this package (licence terms; see exceptions report). Statistics (common pixels, mean, SD, MAE, RMSE, 95th percentile, class disagreement at 7.5/15/25°): tables/inter_dem_slope_difference_summary.csv. Dashed black outline = derived functional footprint (exploratory). Metric scale bar, pyproj graticule and true-north arrow as in Figure B.

### FigC2_validation_dem_vs_lidar_slope_difference

Figure C2. Slope differences at the lidar validation sites on the common valid mask of each pair: global DEM minus lidar-DTM reference (support-matched: the swissALTI3D 2 m / USGS 3DEP 10 m DTM is block-averaged to the 30 m grid before the slope is computed) and NASADEM minus Copernicus. Rows: Davos-Parsenn, Zermatt-Gornergrat (Switzerland), Alta-Snowbird, Park City (USA). Zermatt includes glaciers whose surface changed between the lidar epoch and the DSMs. Global DEMs are DSMs (canopy/buildings included), the reference is a bare-earth DTM; no co-registration was applied. Statistics: tables/inter_dem_slope_difference_summary.csv. Each panel is drawn in its own local UTM zone with an exact metric scale bar, graticule and true-north arrow; dashed outline = derived footprint (exploratory).

### FigD_profile_davos_parsenn

Figure D (Davos-Parsenn). Measured terrain profiles along two grid-aligned 3 km transects through the site centre (rule-based, not selected for agreement; defined in derived/vector/profile_lines.geojson). Reference: swissALTI3D (LN02, 2 m; swisstopo OGD), a bare-earth lidar DTM, shown at native resolution (black) and block-averaged to the 30 m cell (grey) - the SUPPORT-MATCHED reference used in the validation; candidate DEMs: Copernicus GLO-30 DSM (EGM2008) and NASADEM DSM (EGM96), 30 m, bilinearly sampled along the line. Vertical datums are not harmonised, so absolute heights differ by a geoid offset of a few metres; the lower panels remove the median offset along each transect. DSMs include canopy/buildings while the reference is bare earth. No co-registration was applied. Reference epoch: swissALTI3D release year(s) 2023 (25 tiles); Copernicus GLO-30 item date 2021-04-22; NASADEM item date 2000-02-20. Profile values: tables/profile_samples.csv; statistics: tables/profile_summary.csv. Map: exact metric scale bar, pyproj graticule, true-north arrow (grid convergence printed).

### FigE_atl08_track_samples

Figure E. Real ICESat-2 ATL08 v007 terrain segments (100 m along-track; points are the true segment locations) used as a PARTIAL independent reference. Filters fixed before inspecting DEM errors: common = terrain height valid, >= 30 terrain photons, ATM cloud flag <= 2; lenient = height uncertainty < 15 m; strict = < 5 m. Points exist only along the satellite ground tracks (point samples), so they must NOT be read as full-map validation of any slope map. The strict subset is 1-4 % of all segments in every area (table in panel f). Counts in panel titles refer to the displayed extent; full-bbox counts are in tables/atl08_filter_counts.csv. Heights are ellipsoidal and were not geoid-corrected here. Background: hillshade of Copernicus GLO-30 (30 m; grey = outside the packaged windows). Panel (a) covers 76.85-77.20°E, 43.05-43.25°N (UTM 43N); other panels are the lidar validation windows (own local UTM). Exact metric scale bars, graticule, true-north arrows. Vector layers with quality flags: derived/vector/atl08_segments_and_tracks.gpkg. Granule acquisition dates (from granule names): Kazakhstan 2018-11-02 to 2026-07-18 (82 dates); Davos-Parsenn 2018-10-20 to 2026-05-27 (42 dates); Zermatt-Gornergrat 2018-12-22 to 2026-06-14 (47 dates); Alta-Snowbird 2018-11-02 to 2026-07-19 (38 dates); Park City 2019-01-31 to 2026-07-19 (36 dates).
